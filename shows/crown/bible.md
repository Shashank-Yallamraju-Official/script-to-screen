# The Iron Mind

## The show on one page

**Logline.** The power behind a crown vanishes, and eleven houses fight over a seat that was always theatre.

**What it is really about.** A person rebuilding an identity after the one they were handed collapses. Every house is one way that person could run their life.

**The only two decisions.** Everything else in this document follows from them and from the psychology.

1. It is a succession drama in the style of Game of Thrones.
2. It is a metaphor for identity collapse and reformation.

**What actually happened to the person.** The show never states this. It is what every detail has to fit.

| Phase | What happened | In the show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Fusion | They worked for a corporation and believed in it completely | Before the show. Alluded to |
| 2. The act | They did the right thing | Before the show. Alluded to |
| 3. The rupture | The corporation fired them for it | Before the show. Alluded to |
| 4. Collapse of meaning | They questioned everything | **The show starts here** |
| 5. The shift | Their way of thinking changed | The story |
| 6. Building | They look for an identity that is their own | The story. This is where the person is now |
| 7. Settling | Not reached | Open |

The show is called The Iron Mind.

## The houses

Eleven houses, each one personality built around one value. The values are from Schwartz's theory of basic values, which also predicts which houses oppose each other.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Signet | Loyalist | Tradition | Keep things as they have always been | Change |
| Lode | Devotee | Conformity | Find the rightful authority and obey it | Having nobody to follow |
| Hasp | Cautious | Security | Keep harm out | Exposure |
| Mallet | Capable | Achievement | Make things work; repair what is broken | Idleness |
| Bout | Fighter | Power, as face | Never be humiliated again | Being made small |
| Rue | Penitent | Humility | Atone; accept the blame | Going unpunished |
| Plumb | Principled | Universalism | What is right, for everyone, with no exceptions | Compromise |
| Glass | Curious | Self-direction | Understand; keep learning | Being told what to think |
| Brim | Caring | Benevolence | Look after the ones close to it | Someone close going without |
| Whirl | Playful | Hedonism | Enjoy this, now | Being told "later" |
| Cinder | Anarchist | Stimulation | End all rule; never belong to anything again | Being governed |

## Power

Signet sits on the crown, and Hasp and Mallet rule through it. The crown is theatre and always has been, and those two are the only houses near it that know.

**The two powers.** Both were disillusioned long ago and both are honest with themselves about it. They understand that no order is perfect or final, and that things swing in waves.

| House | What it concluded | What it does about it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hasp | Nothing can be trusted, so guard everything | Locks down. The more influence it has, the more the land suffers: this is the drought |
| Mallet | It does not matter who wears the crown, as long as the work goes on | Restores whatever the last lockdown closed |

Neither is trying to win. Each is managing the swing. In psychological terms they are the avoidance system and the approach system.

**The two outcasts.**

- Plumb is too principled to be effective. It allows no exceptions, so it cannot bargain, and nobody with a flaw can stand beside it.
- Glass has seen what Hasp and Mallet have seen and drew a third conclusion: the only thing worth having is understanding. It is not a contender. It has opted out.

**The two that never grew.** Brim and Whirl are the smallest houses. The person's life was the job, so caring for the people close to them and enjoying anything for its own sake were always put off. Neither house has been near the crown, and neither has ever fought for anything.

**What each house believes about the crown.**

| House | Belief |
| --- | --- |
| Signet | It is real, and I serve it |
| Lode | A real one exists somewhere, and I must find it |
| Bout | It was real, and it betrayed us |
| Rue | It was real, and we failed it |
| Plumb | It ought to mean what it says |
| Hasp | It is theatre. Guard against everything |
| Mallet | It is theatre. Keep things working |
| Glass | It is theatre. Learn |

## The land

The land has two directions and each means one thing. North to south is outward to inward. West to east is Mallet's side to Hasp's side. A wall runs across the middle, and everything about where a house sits follows from those three facts. The diagram is [The Two Poles](https://claude.ai/artifact/5JkLv5aqPG6pMrgugyABiG).

Distance is not measured. Two things make places near or far. Roads make them near, because habit carries you along. The Wall makes them far, however close they are on the ground.

| Place | Where | Why there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The outside | Off the top of the map | The corporation was never part of the person |
| Lode | The northern edge, on the road out | It looks outward for someone to follow. It sits between the two poles and points at whichever is up |
| The crown, with Signet on it | Near side, top centre | The old self lived at the border with the outside, and midway between the two powers that ruled through it |
| Mallet | Near side, west, where the roads meet | Its power is movement. Every road is its work |
| Hasp | East, in the Gate | Its power is stopping movement. The Wall is its work |
| Bout | On the Wall, west edge of the Breach | Anger sits on the injury and holds it open |
| Rue | On the Wall, east edge of the Breach | Guilt rubs the break shut, which is Hasp's work done for it. It is the edge nearer Hasp |
| Plumb | Interior, behind the Breach | Its act made the Breach |
| Brim | Interior, near the Breach and Rue | Its only way to Mallet is through the Breach |
| Whirl | Interior, just behind the Gate | The first thing Hasp can reach, so the one Hasp uses |
| Glass | Interior, the deepest place, on Hasp's side | The most the person's own, and the Gate keeps it out |
| Cinder | Interior, bottom centre, directly opposite the crown | It is Signet's mirror, defined by the crown from the other end. Its way to the crown is the Breach |

| Terrain | Whose | Stands for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roads | Mallet's | Habit. They reach the crown, Lode, Mallet and Hasp. A faint one reaches Rue. None crosses the Wall |
| The Wall | Hasp's | What was shut away to fit in |
| The Gate | Hasp's | Fear's permission |
| The Breach | Nobody's | The rupture. Rubble |

| The interior's ground | Nobody's | No roads. Each house there has only its own ground: Plumb's bedrock, Glass's bright flats, Brim's laid table, Whirl's smooth floor |
| Weather | Nobody's | Grief. It falls on both sides of the Wall |

Mallet's reach is long and loose: everything it holds or draws is down a road or through a gap. Hasp's reach is short and tight: everything it holds is next door.

## How the story gets written

The story is found, not written. Each house is given its personality, all of them are set going at once, and we watch what they do. Nothing is turn-based and nothing is scripted.

All numbers are set aside for now: the stats, the distances and the simulation. The society is being established in words first: who each house is, what it values, who holds power over whom, and why. Numbers come back only once that is settled, and they will be read off the society, not the other way round.
