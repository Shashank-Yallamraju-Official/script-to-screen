# Build phases

The restructure and the nudging loop are built in phases. **Every phase ends the same way:**
the reference scene, "The morally righteous location"
(`shows/demo/episodes/study/the-morally-righteous-location.yaml`), is rendered and compared with `s2s parity check`. Unless a
phase says it means to change the scene, its score, picture and sound must be identical to
the phase before. A phase that does mean to change it re-records the reference and says why.

| # | Phase | Reference scene must be | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Parity gate.** `s2s parity record|check`; references for every demo scene | Recorded | Done (`phase-0`) |
| 1 | **Four layers.** `script`, `world`, `performance`, `presentation` as packages that only import downward, enforced by a test. The world changes only through its methods and logs every change with its cause | Identical | Done (`phase-1`), byte-identical video |
| 2 | **Commands and the command line.** 22 commands declared once: read, state, explain, check, still, contact sheet, render, eight changes to a scene, history and undo. JSON out, dry run, addresses, a journal with the writer's words. Changes to entity sheets, the library and show settings are not yet commands | Identical | Done (`phase-2`), byte-identical video |
| 3 | **Identities and the series manifest.** `series.yaml` lists seasons, episodes and segments in order; a scene's id is its identity, wherever its file is. Arcs, addresses, references and output files use ids. Status per scene, with locking. `move`, `add-scene`, `set-status`, and `migrate` (schema 1 to 2) | Identical | Done (`phase-3`), byte-identical video |
| 4 | **The score as the contract.** A scene's performance ends in a `Score` of named tracks (turns, pose, speech, events, focus, cast). Drawing, motion, sound, the ledger, parity and the editor are given a score and a look, and nothing else. A test renders a score that has been through JSON and gets the same picture and sound. `s2s score` exports it | Picture and sound identical. The score's fingerprint was re-recorded on purpose: its tracks were renamed and an event now says it is in passing instead of carrying a volume | Done (`phase-4`), byte-identical video |
| 5 | **Registries.** `vocabulary/` declares every tunable parameter (31: meaning, unit, range, step, where it is written, what changing it redoes, which events show it at work, the words a writer might use) and every observable channel (8: what a tell or a deliberate action may set). The editor's sliders, the checks on entity sheets and actions, and `s2s params` / `s2s channels` are driven by them | Identical | Done (`phase-5`), byte-identical video |
| N1 | **Nudging: fast and safe.** The editor holds no unsaved work: a drag previews in memory, letting go writes that value and journals it. One journal for commands and hand changes, with undo and redo that cover sliders and survive a restart. The editor follows the files. The ledger and earlier scenes are off the nudge path; a look-only slider does not work the scene out again. Release to rebuilt scene: 35 ms (was 78) | Identical | Done (`nudge-1`), byte-identical video |
| N2 | **Nudging: the cascade and `tune:` blocks.** A hand-set value lives in a `tune:` block at its scope (syllable, reaction, turn, scene, entity, tone, sound, show) and outranks the written one. `s2s tune get/set/reset/widen/list`, with addresses for every scope. Per-syllable tunes are tied to the words they were tuned for and set aside if the line changes. The editor writes every nudge this way. A test sets all 14 kinds of tune and checks the story is unchanged | Picture and sound identical. Score fingerprint re-recorded on purpose: each syllable now also carries the value it would have without tuning | Done (`nudge-2`), byte-identical video |
| N3 | **Nudging: whose time is it, breath, the knob.** Every turn is tiled by stretches, each owned by one value (pause, a tell's latency, syllable, breath, beat, a slow reaction, hold); a test checks there are no gaps or overlaps. Breath is its own value, per syllable and show-wide per kind of pause, and tuning it never moves a bubble break. The editor draws the strip; clicking a stretch puts one knob on its value, with scope marks, reset and widen. A knob nudge takes 21 to 29 ms | Picture and sound identical. Score fingerprint re-recorded on purpose: turns now carry their stretches, syllables their kind of pause | Done (`nudge-3`), byte-identical video |
| N4 | **Nudging: audition and compare.** After a nudge the moment replays by itself (the value's stretch with a lead-in and lead-out, set in `show.yaml`). Enter replays it; loop repeats it with a gap. The editor keeps the scene as it stood when the value was picked; B flips between before and after and replays | Identical | Done (`nudge-4`), byte-identical video |
| N5 | **Nudging: in play, widen counts, orphans.** A ranked list of the values at work at any moment (the owner of the time, the syllable's note and stress, what nearby events exercise, their sounds, the tone, the speaker, the voice), each with a moment to replay. In the editor it is a row of icons beside the knob; Tab steps through it. Also `s2s in-play`. Widening reports how many turns or breaths changed. A turn shows when a rewording orphaned its tunes | Identical | Done (`nudge-5`), byte-identical video |
| N6 | **Nudging: picking in the picture, and finding by word.** The renderer reports what is where in a frame; clicking a word in the bubble, the bubble or a token puts the knob on the matching value. `/` finds a value by the words a writer would use, nearest the playhead first, and says so when a match is not at work anywhere in the scene. Also `s2s params --find` | Identical | Done (`nudge-6`), byte-identical video |
| N7 | **A frame cache keyed by content.** A frame is found by its turn's fingerprint (what everyone shows and when, its bubbles, who holds the scene, how each entity stood coming in) and how far into the turn it is, so a turn that only moved in time keeps its frames, and anything seen before (an undo, the other side of a before/after, a nudge and its reverse) is not drawn again. A test checks every cached frame against a fresh draw | Identical | Done (`nudge-7`), byte-identical video |
| M1 | **Music scoring.** A drama step names what happened (who, good or bad for the audience, how surprising, how prominent); a scoring step gives a cue to the moments that are surprising and prominent enough: `turn.bad`, `turn.good` or `question`. The audience's `sympathy` for a character is set in a turn, so a villain's delight scores as bad news. What a cue sounds like is in `library/music.yaml`; `feel.music_volume` sets its level. `s2s drama` explains it. Also fixes a stall when replaying after a sound-only nudge | Picture identical. Sound changes on purpose: the reference scene now has six cues. Fingerprints re-recorded | Done (`music-1`) |
| L1 | **The voice in a bubble.** The dot under the words is replaced: the words wait in grey and take ink as they are said. Chosen from four rendered side by side; the others (`dot`, `trace`, `pill`) stay as `style: {indicator: ...}` in `show.yaml`. Bubbles are tighter without the dot's lane | Sound and score identical. Picture changes on purpose; fingerprints re-recorded | Done (`look-1`) |
| M2 | **A build-up, and music you can see.** The strongest moment of a scene, if strong enough, gets a `rise` that climbs and stops as it lands. On screen the ground line is a string the music plays (it hums toward the spot, then one ripple runs outward, down for bad news and up for good) and the frame closes in during a build-up. Chosen from three rendered side by side; `rings` and `none` remain as `style: {music: ...}` | Sound and picture change on purpose; fingerprints re-recorded | Done (`music-2`) |
| M3 | **Music quality.** Harmony by meaning: figures in scale degrees, voiced in a mode chosen by how good or bad the news is (lydian to locrian), a question with no third, a build-up to the leading tone. Three instruments made of sine partials with shaped envelopes. A mastering chain over three buses: filtering, a dip at 3 kHz, saturation and compression on the music, ducking under speech, one shared room, a soft limiter. Room, ducking and warmth are on the knob. A full mix now takes about 100 ms for an 11-second scene (was 12 ms) | Sound changes on purpose; fingerprints re-recorded. Picture and score identical | Done (`music-3`) |
| L2 | **Gentler motion.** A bubble eases in over 0.3 s from 86% with no bounce, and when its beat ends it fades over 0.4 s while the next one arrives, so bubbles give way to each other instead of being swapped (when the same speaker goes straight on, the old one clears in 0.16 s so two are never read at once). Turning has its own clock (`feel.turn`, 0.5 s) and a smootherstep curve, so the paper thins and comes back gradually. Settle time 0.26 → 0.34 s, overshoot 0.6 → 0.35. The ink in a bubble has a soft edge. New on the knob: `feel.turn`, `feel.bubble_fade` | Score and sound identical. Picture changes on purpose; fingerprints re-recorded | Done |
| C1 | **A camera and places.** The camera is derived from the score by three rules: it never hides a change, it moves only when it must, and a build-up pushes in. A scene opens wide. A turn can ask for a shot with `camera:`. Moves are eased and start a little before the turn they are for (`style: {camera: drift}`; `cut` and `still` are the other choices). A scene names a `place:`; a place is a file of pieces standing along the slots, drawn in layers that slide against each other as the camera moves, in one of three styles (`wash`, `paper`, `line`). Bubbles keep their size at any zoom | Score gains `camera` and `place`; picture changes on purpose; sound identical. Fingerprints re-recorded | Done |
| C2 | **Shots by whose moment it is, in a flat world.** One owner gets a single, two a two-shot, three or more the group; a reaction outside the frame gets its own shot, and until then its line is heard from off screen. A single seats its subject left, centre or right of frame (by default with room on the side they face; `camera: {on, at, zoom, tilt}` on a turn chooses). Whoever a close shot is not of goes out of focus. A hard bad surprise tips the frame, and a build-up pushes in on its subject. A new subject is a cut and a reframing is a move (`style: {camera: film}`). Over-the-shoulder and camera height were built and removed: the world is only ever drawn from the front | Score and picture change on purpose; sound identical. Fingerprints re-recorded | Done |
| E1 | **An episode as one film.** `s2s render --episode` makes one file: scenes and cards in series order, joined by transitions (dip, fade or cut), with one soundtrack. A title card (the show's name takes ink while the theme plays; the cast pop up) and credits (a roll past the cast, with the cast list worked out from the scenes) are segments of kind `card`. A theme in two figures. Places gain props (counter, fridge, thermostat, pedestal, vase, shards, plate), a piece's own colour, `up`, and `like:` for a variant such as night. No shot is shorter than `camera.min_shot`. Episode s01e01 gains three rooms, a closing scene and its cards | The three kitchen scenes now have places, so their pictures change on purpose; sound identical. Fingerprints re-recorded | Done |
| P1, P2 | **Profiles and appraisal.** An entity's sheet gains a profile (four temperament dials, five needs) and bonds; a scene gains `facts:` (what is true, who did it, who knows) and a turn gains `event:` (what happened, to whom, which need). A derivation step works out what the event makes everyone present feel; a written `feel:` wins. `s2s explain` says which it was and why. s01e01 is rewritten as events | Thirteen feelings come out as written; Bix gains two reactions the scripts never gave it, and two strengths move by one. Fingerprints re-recorded | Done (`docs/lld-profiles.md`) |
| P3 | **What if.** `s2s what-if` takes a turn that has an event, or a file with a setup, an event and named variations, and reports how everyone would react in each: the feeling, why, how much would show, and what it inclines them to do next. Variations change who is present, the facts, sides, standing feelings, or anyone's dials, needs and bonds. Nothing is drawn or changed | No scene changes | Done |
| P4 | **Things, regards and placing.** A piece in a place can be named, faced, walked to, and can say which need it `serves`. A regard is what a thing makes someone feel, from their needs or written on their sheet. An entity entering with no `slot` is placed: found in the room, it stands beside what draws it and faces it; arriving later, it comes in by the door or from the side named. `explain` says where and why | "Lights out" writes nobody's place; the kitchen gains a door and the fridge moves. Pictures of the two kitchen scenes change on purpose | Done |
| P5 | **Regards that change, and fire.** A turn can set what a thing means to someone (`regard:`); it is logged, carried into later scenes, and outranks sheet and needs. When a line names the thing (it answers to its `aka` too) or anyone turns to it, the feeling comes up in everyone it means something to, unless the turn already makes them feel something. The camera frames a feeling about a thing with the thing | In "Lights out", Otis reacts to "You ate the dumpling" with nothing written on the turn, and stands on the far side of the fridge from the plate | Done |
| G1 | **Groups, and a story as filters.** A profile gains standing (wealth, status, power), craft and an excuse; pressure and willingness are derived. A tie is trust and rating; the pecking order, factions, the hinge, the weak link and cohesion are read off the ties. A plan routes weight through the ties and cracks at the most strained. Six group tendencies (common enemy, threat rigidity, scapegoating, contagion, conformity, reciprocity). `s2s story` reports, gate by gate, what each decision in `story.yaml` forces, narrows and leaves open | A second show, `shows/heist/`: five decisions force 31 things and leave four open. No scene uses any of it yet | Done (`docs/lld-groups.md`) |
| P3b | **What-ifs as pictures.** `s2s what-if --sheet` stages every column as a still, side by side: a two-turn scene per variation, worked out by the engine with that variation's sheets, drawn at the moment the event has landed. Someone added to the room stands where their regards put them | No scene changes | Done |

## Where the nudging loop ended up

Measured on the demo show, from the action to a rebuilt scene:

| | Prototype | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Preview while dragging | 78 ms (and it rebuilt the audience ledger each time) | 2 ms |
| Letting go: written to the file and journaled | on Save only | 13 ms |
| A knob nudge, written and journaled | n/a | 21 to 29 ms |
| A slider that only changes the look | reworked the whole scene | 7 ms, the scene is not reworked |


The slider panel was removed once the knob could reach every value. Pins were dropped: an
address such as `the-vase:2+6` already names a moment for the assistant.

Left until asked for, with room kept: title cards and skins, themes (a motif for a character or an
idea), a camera that reads the drama track, cached event replay across a long series, the MCP server.
